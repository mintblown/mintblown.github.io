VISUAL STYLE — consistent across all illustrations:

Create a simple black-and-white educational sketch in a hand-drawn classroom / notebook style.

Drawing style:
- pure black ink on a clean white background
- simple, slightly wobbly hand-drawn outlines
- deliberately imperfect linework; lines should look drawn with a black fineliner
- medium to bold line weight, with small natural variations
- simple geometric forms and highly simplified objects
- people are minimalist stick figures with round heads and very few facial features
- friendly, playful, but not childish or cartoonishly exaggerated
- no photorealism, no 3D rendering, no gradients
- no grey fills or large solid black areas
- use sparse diagonal hatching only where shading, material or depth needs clarification
- motion is shown with simple arrows, curved arrows, dashed trajectories or a few motion lines
- forces and physical quantities use clean vector arrows where appropriate
- generous white space; avoid unnecessary decorative details

Composition:
- diagram-like and immediately readable
- objects clearly separated
- side views preferred for physical situations
- consistent perspective and scale where objects are compared
- if multiple situations are shown, arrange them as clearly separated panels
- panel borders may be slightly irregular and hand-drawn
- prioritize physical and conceptual correctness over artistic composition
- all important spatial relationships must be unambiguous

Typography:
- avoid text unless explicitly requested
- when text is necessary, use short handwritten-looking black labels
- mathematical symbols and arrows must remain clearly legible

Overall impression:
A physics teacher's carefully drawn black-fineline sketch on a white sheet of paper: reduced, charmingly imperfect, visually calm, scientifically clear, and suitable for worksheets and presentation slides.
